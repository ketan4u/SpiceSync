Fumble:

A dating app for India from India, centered around a thing that all Indians love i.e. Food. 
The goal is to find a person's psychological match.

Problem that we are solving:

We take that saying literally with this app: 'the way to someone's heart goes through their stomach). Food is an important part of a person's life and it should be a criteria when two people match. It is often seen that good matches have similar taste buds. So we are using that aspect in our app.

People rarely find good matches, now this might be because people don't know what they want from dating and so we need to create an app that helps them understand what they actually want. And also another side of not finding good matches is that the algorithm does not show good user cards too often, that can be solved using an additional layer of matching i.e. food.

A dating app for all Indians wherever they are. Indians sitting in London can connect with people in Bangalore or Delhi. Your soulmate can be anywhere in the world

A basic assumption that people can't always be articulate about what they are like and what they want from dating. It is through some psychological and other questions the app can learn what they would actually be interested in.


[The Marriage matching feature can be implemented at a later stage but it is a powerful opportunity and a good differentiator: 
Hinge is a beautiful app that has a good matching algorithm, but it leaves some important problems aside, problems that mostly Indians face. It is location based, and it does not have a dedicated marriage matching in a way that most Indians and their parents like. Although location is important for dating, it is not exactly so for marriage. Many people living in small cities change their location to big cities because they would like to shift to these cities after marriage]

While Registering:

Section 1:
Basic Info - 
photos(at least one self photo which can be selfie verified and a photo of the food they really liked), 
education, job, height, non-negotiables, 
dating intentions( Marriage, long term, short term, casual, exploring; this can be multiple select except the last one i.e. if exploring is selected no other can be selected so it will have a radio button and others will have checkboxes, if exploring is selected all others will automatically be deselected), preferred city that you would like to settle to after marriage and city you were born in if marriage is chosen as dating intention, 
red flags that you most certainly avoid, 
past relationship learnings(if any, can be skipped for MVP), 
political views(if any), 
some prompts regarding their day to day work and interests which they can choose to answer, 
Location and a checkbox for asking to find good matches in farther locations if the match score is good.
If you were not doing this job, what would you rather do? should be selected from a list which opens as suggestions to typed text. a checkbox for disabling that list with text 'I'm happy in what I do'. People can choose to hide this detail but this is mandatory as it might help in matching.
In my free days, I find myself doing?(a text based quetion)

Section 2:
A/B tests using food photos to enquire the food preferances of a person. The photo selected will be present as one of two options in the next round and the food photo that remains at the end of the test will be the food that represents the person. These are likely choices that a person would rather make with regard to food. This can go 6-8 rounds deep. All choices can be stored or a rough taste idea can be created from this.

Section 3 (this section can be skipped altogether and can be filled at a later stage or later therough prompts shown while swiping):
Some simple psychological situational questions which try to make a map of personality type of a person. 


Matching algorithm:

Among people who want similar dating outcomes i.e. people with similar dating intentions:
1st priority: Food preferance matching 
2nd priority: Non-negotiables should not be present
3rd priority: psychological compatibility
4th priority: if for Marriage, a quick kundli matching can also be done.

A match score will be assigned to two users. Whichever users have match scores more than 70% with a particular user will be shown at the top in the Explore section of the app to that user. 

Look and feel of the app:

The Explore screen will have cards like that of Hinge and will show the following info in an aesthetic way:
Food preferances will be shown as clubbed into cousine & spicy level. e.g. a user can have this value as North Indian, Mild spicy, another user can have this value as Chinese, Extra Spicy.
A photo of them and a photo of the food that represents them.
Current location, and if Marriage is the intention which city they will be willing to move to.
If the match score is more than 85% an alert will be shown at the top of the profile, that 'We think you will be a good match, you should try to meet.'
